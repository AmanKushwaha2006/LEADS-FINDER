# 🚀 PlacementScout AI

> AI-powered recruitment intelligence platform for Training & Placement Cells.

PlacementScout AI helps college TNP Cells discover companies that are actively hiring, identify recruiters, manage outreach, and automate repetitive lead generation workflows.

---

## 📌 Problem

Training & Placement Cells spend countless hours manually:

- Searching LinkedIn for hiring posts
- Tracking companies visiting other colleges
- Finding HRs and Talent Acquisition professionals
- Collecting recruiter information
- Maintaining Excel sheets
- Following hiring trends

This process is repetitive, time-consuming, and difficult to scale.

---

## 💡 Solution

PlacementScout AI automates the recruitment research process by helping TNP teams:

- 🔍 Discover companies actively hiring
- 👥 Identify recruiters and Talent Acquisition professionals
- 📊 Track hiring trends
- 📈 Manage recruiter pipelines
- 🤖 Automate repetitive lead generation tasks
- 📑 Generate recruiter databases and reports
